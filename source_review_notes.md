# Source Review Notes

## Mason Nguyen GEO website

**Reviewed:** 2026-08-13

The public homepage presents a professional services offer for GEO audits and lists service-delivery components such as Share of Model probes, schema coverage analysis, llms.txt review, sameAs graph checks, and a Signal Score report. A site search for “case” returned no public case-study or documented outcome record. The site is therefore suitable as first-party methodology and ecosystem context, but not as a published client-results record without a separately approved source, scope, reporting window, and publication authorization.
